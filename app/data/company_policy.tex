\section{Overview \& Customer Support Operations}
Welcome to NovaTech Solutions. Our automated voice assistant, Nova, works alongside our human support team to provide 24/7 customer service. This policy document governs all purchases, order modifications, returns, warranties, and service level agreements.

\section{Order Management \& Status Inquiries}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
    \item \textbf{Order Status Inquiries:} Customers may check the status of any order by providing their numeric Order ID to Nova or a support agent.
    \item \textbf{Processing Orders:} Orders in the \textit{Processing} state can be cancelled or updated directly over the phone or via voice assistant.
    \item \textbf{Shipped Orders:} Once an order status transitions to \textit{Shipped}, it cannot be cancelled or modified over the phone. Customers must wait for delivery and initiate a return.
    \item \textbf{Address Changes:} Shipping addresses can only be updated while the order is in \textit{Order Placed} or \textit{Processing} status.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Returns, Refunds \& Replacement Policy}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
    \item \textbf{30-Day Money-Back Guarantee:} All physical products and software licenses purchased from NovaTech come with a 30-day full refund guarantee from the date of delivery.
    \item \textbf{Return Eligibility:} To qualify for a refund, items must be in original packaging. Proof of purchase or Order ID is required.
    \item \textbf{Damaged or Defective Items:} If a package arrives crushed, damaged, or with missing components, NovaTech provides a free replacement with priority shipping upon reporting.
    \item \textbf{Non-Refundable Items:} Digital download keys that have been activated or customized items are non-refundable after 30 days.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Technical Support \& Operating Hours}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
    \item \textbf{Human Support Hours:} Our human technical support agents are available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST.
    \item \textbf{Automated Voice Agent:} Nova AI is available 24/7 for order tracking, cancellations, address updates, and damage reports.
    \item \textbf{Manager Escalations:} If a customer issue cannot be resolved by Nova or requires human intervention, an urgent support ticket is logged with a guaranteed 1-hour callback window during support hours.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Software Upgrades \& Licensing Terms}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
    \item \textbf{Premium Tier Upgrades:} All Premium Tier subscribers receive lifetime free upgrades to all major software updates.
    \item \textbf{Basic Tier Upgrades:} Basic Tier users may upgrade to the latest major software release for a flat upgrade fee of \$15.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Security \& Account Privacy}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
    \item \textbf{Password Security:} NovaTech does not store plain-text passwords. All credentials are encrypted using industry-standard hashing.
    \item \textbf{Manual Resets Disallowed:} Support agents and Nova AI cannot manually view or reset passwords over the phone. Customers must use the self-service password reset portal on the official website.
\end{enumerate}
